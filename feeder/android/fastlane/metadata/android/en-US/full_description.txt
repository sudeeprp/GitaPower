The Bhagavad Gita is a conversation between Lord Krishna and Arjuna just before a battle. This conversation is a journey through our self, purpose, and relationship with the Lord.

A thousand years ago, Sri Ramanuja made a consistent, well-knit commentary along the philosophy of Vishishtadvaita. This app presents the commentary, translated into English.
